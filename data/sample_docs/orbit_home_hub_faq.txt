Orbit Home Hub - Product FAQ

Setup
Plug the Orbit Home Hub into a power outlet and wait for the light ring to pulse blue. Open the Orbit app, tap "Add device" and scan the QR code on the bottom of the hub. The hub connects to 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks. Setup normally takes under five minutes.

Factory reset
To reset the Orbit Home Hub to factory settings, press and hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the light ring turns red, then release. All paired devices and Wi-Fi credentials are erased. A reset cannot be undone.

Supported protocols
The hub supports Zigbee 3.0, Z-Wave, Thread and Matter. Up to 150 devices can be paired to a single hub.

Warranty
The Orbit Home Hub comes with a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but not water damage or unauthorised modification. Warranty claims require proof of purchase.

Battery backup
The built-in backup battery keeps the hub online for up to 8 hours during a power outage.

Troubleshooting
A solid red light ring means the hub has lost its internet connection. Check your router and restart the hub by unplugging it for 10 seconds. A blinking yellow light ring means a firmware update is in progress; do not unplug the hub during an update. A white light ring means the hub is ready.

Firmware updates
The hub installs firmware updates automatically overnight between 02:00 and 04:00 local time. Updates can be postponed for up to 7 days in the Orbit app under Settings. Security updates cannot be postponed.

Power
The hub uses a 12 V power adapter, which is included in the box. Idle power consumption is about 6 W.

App compatibility
The Orbit app requires iOS 15 or later, or Android 10 or later. The hub can also be controlled through Matter-compatible apps from other vendors.

Privacy
The hub has no microphone and no camera. Device data is stored locally on the hub by default. Cloud backup is optional and can be switched off in the app.

Returns
Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if they are in original condition. After 30 days, only warranty claims are accepted.
